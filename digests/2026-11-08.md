## Shipped

- **Billing API:** failed charges keep the processor's response code for support.[^prs-mock-16]
- **Customer Dashboard:** the webhook settings page lists 7 days of delivery attempts and resends failed events.[^prs-mock-12]
- **Tracking Events:** raw carrier events are kept for 14 days so dropped updates can be replayed.[^prs-mock-11]
- **Webhooks:** the delivery queue is partitioned by shipment, which fixes out-of-order events.[^prs-mock-5][^issues-mock-4]

## Incidents

- 4 November, high, 35 minutes: purchases for one carrier failed during its outage, until someone set the carrier override.[^incidents-mock-4]
- 2 November, medium, 20 minutes: a third of shipment creates failed after a migration added a required column with no default.[^incidents-mock-6]

## Decisions

- Proposed, not decided: fail over to a backup carrier for labels. It waits on who pays when the backup carrier costs more.[^decisions-2026-11-03-carrier-failover-for-labels]

## Drift from spec

- Billing API retries stop after 3 attempts, where Idempotent charges requires 5 with backoff.[^systems-billing-api]
- Webhook retries stop after 5 attempts, where Webhook delivery guarantees requires 8 over 24 hours.[^systems-webhooks]
- Carrier rates are cached for 60 minutes, where Rate quotes under 300 ms requires 15.[^systems-rates-engine]
- The dashboard delivery log shows 7 days with a status filter, where its PRD requires 30 days and an event type filter.[^systems-customer-dashboard]
