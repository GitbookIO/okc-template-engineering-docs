# Systems

- [Billing API](billing-api.md): Charges customers for labels and retries failed charges.
- [Customer Dashboard](customer-dashboard.md): Web app where customers manage shipments, billing and webhooks.
- [Label Service](label-service.md): Buys shipping labels from carriers and renders them.
- [Ledger](ledger.md): Double-entry record of every balance change and charge.
- [Rates Engine](rates-engine.md): Quotes carrier rates for a shipment and caches them.
- [Shipments API](shipments-api.md): Public API to create, list and track shipments.
- [Tracking Events](tracking-events.md): Collects carrier tracking updates into shipment timelines.
- [Webhooks](webhooks.md): Delivers shipment events to customer endpoints, with retries.
